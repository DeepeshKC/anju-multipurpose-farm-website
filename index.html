WELCOME TO ANJU FARM

A family tradition, growing with nature.

Located in Basdol, Banepa, Anju Multipurpose Agriculture Farm is a family-run farm built around agriculture, livestock and a connection to the land.

We welcome visitors to discover our farm, enjoy traditional home-cooked meals and experience the everyday rhythms of Nepali village life.

A HOME AWAY FROM THE CITY
STAY WITH OUR FAMILY

Not a hotel.
A home. A family.
A village.

Experience a traditional homestay in our family home. Enjoy a simple, welcoming place to rest, share meals and discover the everyday life of a working farm.

  • Traditional family-home accommodation
  • Home-cooked Nepali meals
  • Farm and livestock activities
  • A personal introduction to village life

Accommodation is in our existing family home, not a resort or purpose-built hotel. Contact us to discuss availability and rates.
